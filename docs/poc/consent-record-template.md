# Voice participation and consent record (template)

Keep one signed copy per participant, outside the repository. This is a template for the pilot, not legal
advice: have counsel review it, and the provider's data terms, before involving anyone beyond a willing
colleague. Do not upload anything until this is signed.

**Participant:** ______________________  **Date:** ____________  **Operator:** ______________________

**What will happen**
1. A short recording of your singing or speaking voice will be stored (encrypted) by DeenVoice.
2. The recording will be sent to **Mureka (a third-party music AI service)** to create a reusable
   "voice profile". Mureka may keep or use voice data under its own terms and has said that uploaded voice
   data may be used to improve its algorithms, and that generated music "may contain your unique vocal
   characteristics". Deleting your voice in DeenVoice may **not** remove Mureka's copy; we will ask Mureka to
   delete it, but we cannot confirm that they do.
3. Songs will be generated in a voice similar to yours, from lyrics provided for this trial, and will be
   played to the people running and evaluating the trial.
4. Nothing will be published or shared outside the trial without your separate written permission.

**Your choices**
- You may withdraw at any time. On request we will delete the recording, the voice profile and the songs
  made with it from DeenVoice, and ask the provider to delete theirs.
- You may refuse any use above and still take part in other ways.

**Declaration**
I am an adult. I have read and understood the above. The recording is of my own voice. I agree to take part.

Signature: ______________________   Date: ____________

*(If someone else is giving permission for a voice that is not theirs, do not proceed: voices of third parties,
and of anyone who cannot consent, are out of scope for this trial.)*

The text a user accepts in the app is served by the backend (`GET /v1/consent-statements`) and recorded with
its hash; this paper form is for the trial and does not replace it.
